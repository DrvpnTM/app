# How to buy a VPN subscription + meet Dr VPN

A VPN is an everyday tool for many people: free access to the internet, more safety on public Wi-Fi and better privacy. The market is crowded, so here are the key points to check before you buy, and an introduction to the open-source **Dr VPN** app.

## What is a VPN subscription?

When you buy a subscription you get access to servers. Usually you receive one of these:

- **Subscription link:** one address that contains the whole server list. Import it once and tap “update” to get new servers when the seller changes them.
- **Single config:** a link starting with `vless://`, `vmess://`, `trojan://` or `ss://` — one server.
- **QR code:** the same link as an image for quick scanning.

## 7 things to check before buying

1. **Protocols** – newer ones such as **VLESS + Reality** or **Hysteria2** are usually more stable and harder to detect.
2. **Free trial or a short plan** – quality differs by city and ISP; test on your own connection first.
3. **Data and duration** – what happens when the data runs out? Can you see your usage?
4. **Simultaneous devices** – check how many devices can connect at once.
5. **Number and location of servers** – more locations mean a backup when one is slow.
6. **Responsive support** – a good seller answers quickly and replaces broken servers.
7. **Beware of “very cheap” and “unlimited”** – good servers cost money. Never enter bank details outside an official payment gateway.

## Meet Dr VPN

**Dr VPN** is a **free and open-source** Android VPN app built to be simple: anyone should be able to connect in seconds.

- **Simple like Hiddify:** the home screen is one big connect button plus your subscription and server.
- **Powerful like v2rayNG:** built on v2rayNG and Xray-core; supports VLESS, VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks, Hysteria2 and WireGuard.
- **Only working servers:** servers are pinged automatically and dead ones are hidden.
- **Country flags** so you know where you are connected.
- **Per-app proxy, several languages, dark mode.**
- **Open source (GPL-3.0):** anyone can review the code on GitHub.

### Connect in 4 steps

1. Install Dr VPN and choose your country and language.
2. Copy your subscription link, tap **+** and choose “Import from clipboard”.
3. Pick a server in the **Proxies** tab.
4. Go to **Home** and tap the big button. When it turns green you are connected ✅
